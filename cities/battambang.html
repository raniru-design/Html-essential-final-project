Battambang

Battambang is known for its well-preserved French colonial architecture, quiet riverside charm, and thriving creative community. Surrounding the town are lush rice paddies, historic temples, and unique attractions like the famous local bamboo train.

Personal Reflection

Battambang feels like taking a deep breath. With its open rice fields, old colonial corners, and slow-paced street life, it connects you to a simpler, more patient side of the country. It reminds you of the warmth of local communities and how important it is to keep life balanced.
