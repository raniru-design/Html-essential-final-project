Siemreap: An acient civilization embeded into a new city.

Siem Reap is the cultural capital of Cambodia and the gateway to the UNESCO World Heritage site of Angkor Wat. It blends ancient history with a lively town center, boasting vibrant night markets, cozy cafes, and the famous Pub Street.

Personal Reflection

Siem Reap hits differently because it forces you to slow down. Standing in front of Angkor Wat or just walking around town gives you a quiet sense of pride. It's less about being a tourist and more about stepping back from the city noise to connect with where we come from. It feels like grounding yourself in history.
