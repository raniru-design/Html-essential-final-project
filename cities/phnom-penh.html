Phnom Penh: Camboida's Famous Capital

Phnom Penh is Cambodia's bustling capital city, located at the junction of the Mekong and Tonle Sap rivers. It is the country's economic and political center, featuring landmarks like the Royal Palace alongside modern skyscrapers and a growing food scene.

Personal Reflection

Phnom Penh is where the daily grind happens. Between navigating traffic, handling school or work, and watching new buildings go up every few months, it keeps you on your toes.
